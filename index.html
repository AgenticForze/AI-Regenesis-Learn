Why it's built this way

01

Dual-Altitude Explanation

A plain-language pass and a rigorous deep-dive pass for every concept — read only what your role needs.

02

Comparative Framing

Every concept sits next to the two or three things people confuse it with, and a straight answer on which to use when.

03

Domain-Grounded

Four worked use cases apiece in Finance, Healthcare, and Telecom — never toy examples.

04

Architecture-First

Anything with moving parts gets a labeled reference architecture diagram, not just prose.
